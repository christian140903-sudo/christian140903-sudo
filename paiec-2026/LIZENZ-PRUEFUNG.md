# Licence check of `curation/` and `audit/` (protocol)

Scope: which derived tables may be published in this repository, and why. Reviewed by the author (not by a lawyer); this is not legal advice.
Derived from: measurement-db revision `54074453da7b9911be0f48e4c560be547bc84d21` (gated dataset; the curated collection is described by its
card as CC BY-SA 4.0 [Vermutung: card wording as recorded by the author, re-check before release]; each benchmark keeps its own upstream
terms; the dataset README asks not to redistribute the data).

Rule applied: only identifiers, hashes, counts, rates, model/benchmark names and short categorical labels are published; no item text,
no responses, no raw data. Attribution: the tables derive from the AI Measurement Data Bank (aims-foundations/measurement-db, revision above).
